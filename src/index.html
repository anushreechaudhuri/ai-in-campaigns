I hypothesize that non-incumbent campaigns, losing campaigns, and House campaigns (and possibly Republican campaigns, because they are anecdotally friendlier to AI use or at least less morally opposed) will have higher AI %. This is because less well-resourced campaigns, often with few or zero paid staff, are more likely to now depend on AI tools. Initial numbers are loosely supporting this, but I'm curious if the correlations will hold up across the full dataset.
